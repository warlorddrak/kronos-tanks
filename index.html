▶ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

How does it work?

Per boss kill, at most one player is considered the "tank":

  1. The player having taken most "damage taken" plus "damage absorbed".
  2. Only if that player did not die. If that player died, no eligible tank is tracked for that fight.

The "Top 50 Leaderboard" ranks tanks by granting points for each top 10 boss result (rank #1 10 points, rank #2 9 points, ..., rank #10 1 point).

How often does it update?

Due to using free cloud tiers, it updates on an irregular schedule (circa 1-4 times a day) or when I manually trigger it.
